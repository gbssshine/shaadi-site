Privacy policy

What Shaadi Parrot collects, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information.

Last updated: 2026-10-11

Overview

Shaadi Parrot is a matrimony app for people looking to marry and for their families, with kundli matching, the panchang and Mithu, our astrology parrot. This policy explains what data we collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information.

1) Data we collect

  • Account: your phone number, or the e-mail of the Google account you sign in with. We use it for sign-in and safety and never show it to other users. When you sign in, we record that you confirmed you are 18 or older and accepted the Terms and this policy, with the date and the version.
  • Rishta profile (biodata): name, gender, date of birth and age, height, photos, a few lines about you, religion, mother tongue and languages, marital status and, if you were married before, whether you have children, education, work, food habits, city, family details (family type and values, parents’ work, brothers and sisters, family city) and who the profile is for.
  • Only if you choose: community, caste, sub-caste, gotra and Manglik status. They show on your rishta profile to members; “Prefer not to say” or leaving them empty keeps them off your biodata.
  • Annual income range (optional): shown to everyone who opens your full profile, or only to accepted interests, as you choose. Our moderators can see it when they review a report. Remove it any time by clearing the field, or delete your account.
  • Profiles made for someone else: if you make a profile for your son, daughter, brother, sister, relative or friend, we record your relation to them and your declaration that they know about the profile and agree to it, with the date and the text version. If that person does the face check, we also record their own agreement and its date.
  • My family: the names, relations and birth details of the people you add, used for their kundli, their day and good dates.
  • Partner preferences: what you are looking for, such as age, religion, mother tongue, caste, Manglik, marital status, children, food, education, work, height, income and area. See “How matching works”.
  • Astrology: your birth date and, if you add them, your birth time (or roughly when: morning, afternoon, evening or night) and birth place. They make your kundli, kundli matches, Today’s rishtas, the panchang and your horoscope. Members see your age. Members who check a kundli match with you see the result worked out from your birth details: the guna score, your Moon sign and nakshatra, and whether a Manglik dosha shows. They never see your birth date, time or place.
  • Festivals and region: the festivals you celebrate, your mother tongue or region, and how much astrology you want (“Kundli, your way”). They are used only to pick your festivals, fasts and calendar and what Mithu shows you. They are never shown on a profile, and you can change or remove them in Me. (Religion and mother tongue in your rishta biodata do show on your profile.)
  • Tests: your answers to Mithu’s personality tests, if you take them, for your results and for Today’s rishtas.
  • Location: if you allow it, your device location sets your city and the distance shown to others. We store it only rounded to about 2 km, in your account and on your profile card; members see only your city and an approximate distance, never your exact position.
  • Photos and photo privacy: every photo is checked automatically for safety (Google Cloud Vision). If you keep your photos private, members you are matched with or have sent an interest to see them; other members see a blurred version, made on our server. Turning it on doesn’t take back photos people have already seen.
  • Biodata PDF: made on our server from your profile when you ask, sent to your phone, not kept.
  • Interests, chats and safety: the interests you send and accept, your chats, blocks and reports. Chats and reports may be processed to detect abuse, spam, scams and rule-breaking.
  • Rishta notes: notes, tags and reminders you keep about rishtas, and the notes Mithu writes for you, stay in your account. Only your account sees them, and they are deleted with it (never archived).
  • Activity status: while the App is open, people you are matched with can see that you’re online, or roughly when you were last active. Nobody else can see it.
  • Purchases: Parrot+, reading credits and other items you buy, and their balances. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details.
  • Usage, device and diagnostics: which screens you open and key actions (for example interests and messages sent, never message text), purchase outcomes, crash reports, device identifiers and notification tokens, collected with Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics and linked to your account ID so we can fix problems and improve the App.
  • Advertising: we show rewarded ads (which you choose to watch, for example to get a reward) and sponsored cards in Quick swipe (labelled “Sponsored”, with Google’s AdChoices icon) through Google AdMob, which uses your device’s advertising ID. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first (Google’s consent form); you can change your choice anytime in Settings › Account › Privacy choices. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. Parrot+ removes sponsored cards and offers to watch ads.
  • In-app ratings: if you rate the App when Mithu asks, we keep the stars you give with your account ID, so the team can see how the App is doing.

2) Face verification

Face verification is optional and happens only with your consent. The front camera opens in the App and takes three photos while you follow a few moves. From them and from your profile photos we make face templates (numbers that describe a face) to check it’s you in your photos and that one person has one verified account. Google Cloud Vision reads the head position and checks the photos are safe; the templates are made and compared on our own server and never shared. Usually no one sees the photos; if the automatic check isn’t sure, an authorized team member compares them with your profile photos (every look is logged). Two photos are deleted right after the check; while you’re verified we keep one straight photo and your face template; a check that doesn’t pass is deleted within 30 days. For a profile made by a family member, the person in the profile does the face check, and it also records their agreement to the profile. Remove your verification anytime in Me: tap your Verified badge, then Manage › Remove my verification (only the record of your consent stays until you delete your account). Never used for ads, never sold.

3) Mithu and our AI provider

Mithu’s chat answers, daily horoscopes and forecasts, kundli match readings and the notes on Today’s rishtas are written with the help of our AI provider, DeepSeek, whose servers are outside India (they may be in China). To write them, our server sends DeepSeek only what the answer needs:

  • your message, and the parts of your own profile that help the answer, such as first name, age, gender, marital status, city, languages, about you, interests, education and work and, for kundli questions, your birth date, time and place;
  • the computed kundli and horoscope facts;
  • for a kundli match reading, the two names and the computed match;
  • the names, relations, ages and Moon signs of the people in My family (their ages, never their birth dates);
  • today’s festival or fast for the faiths you chose.

When you ask Mithu about a match, the details on that person’s profile card and your computed kundli match are used the same way, never their birth date, time or place. To pick Today’s rishtas and say why, the AI gets first names, ages, cities, part of the “about” text, shared interests, how your life plans and test answers compare, and the kundli result. We never send the caste, sub-caste, gotra, Manglik status or income from your biodata to the AI. The kundli result it gets is worked out from birth details and can say whether a Manglik dosha shows. Your chats with Mithu, and a short summary he keeps of them so his answers stay personal, are stored in your account and deleted with it. Please don’t share passwords, bank details or other sensitive information with Mithu.

4) How matching works

Your partner preferences rank the people Mithu shows you in Rishtas: the best fits come first. A preference you mark “Must have” hides people outside it both ways: you don’t see them, and they don’t see you. Other members never see your preferences; at most they see a short line that they fit what your family asked for. Rishtas shows only people looking to marry: grooms 21 or older and brides 18 or older. Profiles made by family members, married people and kundli-only accounts never appear in Quick swipe.

5) How we use data

To run the App: show your rishta profile to members, rank Rishtas and Today’s rishtas, work out kundlis, matches, the panchang and horoscopes, deliver interests, chats and notifications, make the biodata PDF, keep members safe (photo checks, the face check, reports and moderation), prevent fraud and ban evasion, process purchases, fix problems and improve the App, and meet our legal duties.

6) Who sees what, and sharing

  • Members: signed-in members see your rishta profile: name, age, photos (blurred if you keep them private), city, about you and the biodata details you added. Your income shows only as you choose. Your phone number, e-mail, birth date, time and place, partner preferences and notes are never shown. Members who check a kundli match with you see the result worked out from your birth details: the guna score, your Moon sign and nakshatra, and whether a Manglik dosha shows. They never see your birth date, time or place. People you block can’t open your profile.
  • Our team: an authorized team member can see a profile, the reports about it and the reported messages when reviewing a report.
  • Service providers, each with only what it needs: Google Firebase / Google Cloud (hosting, sign-in, database, storage, notifications, analytics, crash reports, image safety checks and the face check’s head-position reading), Cloudflare (delivering profile photos quickly), Google AdMob (ads), Google Play (payments) and DeepSeek (Mithu’s AI answers).
  • The law: we may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect people’s safety and rights.

7) Where your data is processed

Shaadi Parrot is operated by DeLuca Family Universe LLC, a company in the United States. Your data is stored and processed on Google Cloud and Firebase servers in the United States and the European Union. Photos in the 180-day archive (section 8) are kept on Google Cloud in India (Mumbai). Mithu’s AI answers are processed by DeepSeek, whose servers may be located outside India, including in China. We transfer data outside India only as the law allows, share with each provider only what it needs for its task, and protect your data as described in this policy wherever it is processed.

8) How long we keep it

  • We keep your data while your account exists, and only as long as the service, safety and the law need it.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photos, biodata, family, interests, matches, chats, rishta notes, reminders and settings.
  • As Indian law requires, for 180 days after you delete your account we keep your phone number, e-mail, name and photos in a closed archive that only our safety team can open; then they are deleted automatically. Photos our moderators remove are kept the same way for 180 days.
  • Rishta notes and reminders are never archived. Chats are kept only when they are part of an open report, or belong to an account suspended or banned for breaking our rules: then for up to 180 days for the safety review, and then deleted.
  • A one-way hash of your phone number (not the number itself) is kept for 180 days, so that a ban, or free allowances already used, carry over if the number signs up again.
  • Face verification data is kept as described in section 2. The biodata PDF is not kept.

9) Your choices and rights

You can:

  • edit your biodata, preferences and family in the App, and clear any optional field;
  • keep your photos private, and choose who sees your income;
  • change device permissions (location, camera, notifications) in your device settings;
  • remove your face verification (section 2);
  • change your ad choices in Settings › Account › Privacy choices;
  • delete your account in Settings › Delete my account (see section 8).

Is there a profile of you that you didn’t agree to? Report it with “Made without this person’s OK”, or write to support@shaadiparrot.com (no account needed), or use shaadiparrot.com/help.html#remove-profile.

10) Adults only, and children’s data

The App is only for adults: you must be 18 or older, and a groom must be 21 or older to have a rishta profile. If you think someone under 18 is using the App, report their profile or write to support@shaadiparrot.com right away; a report that someone looks under 18 hides the profile at once while we check. Children never have profiles. Parents can add their children to My family for their kundli, baby names and good dates; you do this as their parent or guardian, and it stays in your account.

11) Grievances and your rights in India

If you are in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you the right to access a summary of your data, correct or update it, have it erased, withdraw consent and nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. You can edit your profile in the App and delete your account in Settings › Delete my account.

For any other request or complaint about your data, write to our Grievance Officer, Arsen Khasiev, at support@shaadiparrot.com with the subject “Privacy grievance”. We acknowledge requests within 24 hours and resolve them within 7 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

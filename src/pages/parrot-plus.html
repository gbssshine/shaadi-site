How billing works

Simple, and all through your Google Play account.

  • Parrot+ is billed monthly by Google Play and renews automatically until you cancel.
  • Cancel any time in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Parrot+ stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play first.
  • Refunds follow Google Play’s refund policies, except where the law requires otherwise.
  • Changed phones? Use Restore purchases in the app.

A very old idea, on a new phone

On a street corner in Chennai or Varanasi, a parrot astrologer opens a little cage. The parrot hops out, walks along a row of envelopes and pulls one out with its beak. Your fortune is inside. People have been lining up for that moment for generations.

Dating apps went the other way: endless profiles, endless swiping, nobody choosing anything. We wanted the feeling of that parrot back. Someone picks a few cards for you, and you open one, with a little ceremony.

So Shaadi Parrot has Mithu. Every morning he picks three people for you, reads their fate, and lets you decide. He also reads your stars, helps you find the right words, and keeps the whole thing a bit more fun than it has any right to be.

    Your profile, or your child’s

    A good profile takes a few minutes. You decide how much to share.

    Photos and basics

    Photos, name, age, height and a few lines about you. Every photo gets an automatic check before it goes live, and you can keep photos private.

    Who it’s for

    Yourself, your son or daughter, a brother, sister, relative or friend, with their agreement. Profiles made by family say who manages them.

    The biodata

    Religion, mother tongue, marital status, education, work, food and family details: what families read first.

    Faith and community

    Caste, gotra and Manglik status are optional, and shown only if you choose.

    Birth details

    Your birth date makes your kundli. Add birth time and place for an exact Guna Milan and Manglik check.

    Private by default

    Your phone number is never shown. People see your city and a rough distance, not where you are.

    Interests, crowns and matches

    The familiar parts, done simply.

    Interest

    Send an interest to a rishta you like. If they accept, it’s a match and you can chat. Seeing and accepting interests is free; Parrot+ removes the daily limit on the ones you send.

    Crown

    A crown tells someone you’re really interested and helps you stand out.

    Boost

    A boost puts your profile in front of more people for a while. Good for Sunday evenings.

    Your profile, your rules

    A good profile takes a few minutes. You decide how much to share.

    Photos and basics

    Your photos, name, age, height and a short bio. Every photo gets an automatic check before it goes live.

    What you’re looking for

    A serious long-term partner, open to both, dating to see where it goes, friends, or not sure yet. You meet people who want the same.

    Interests and lifestyle

    Your interests, languages and lifestyle, so conversations start with something you share.

    Faith and community

    Religion and community help us show better matches. They’re optional, and only shown if you choose.

    Birth details

    Your birth date powers your horoscope. Add birth time and place for a more precise reading, if you like.

    Private by default

    Your phone number is never shown. People see your city and a rough distance, not where you are.

    Likes, crowns and matches

    The familiar parts, done simply.

    Like

    Like someone’s profile. If they like you back, it’s a match and you can chat. Free members get a daily number of likes; Parrot+ removes the limit.

    Crown

    A crown is a super like. It tells someone you’re really interested and helps you stand out.

    Boost

    A boost puts your profile in front of more people for a while. Good for Sunday evenings.

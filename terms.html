Summary

Shaadi Parrot is a matrimony app: it helps people who are looking to marry, and their families, find a rishta, with kundli matching, the panchang and Mithu, our astrology parrot. It is not a dating app. These Terms govern your use of Shaadi Parrot (the “App”), operated by DeLuca Family Universe LLC (“we”, “us”). By creating an account or using the App, you agree to these Terms and our Privacy Policy and confirm that you are at least 18 years old.

1) Who can use Shaadi Parrot

  • You must be 18 or older to use the App.
  • Rishtas are only for people looking to marry. Don’t use the App for dating, casual relationships or business. The same rules apply in Quick swipe.
  • A groom must be 21 or older and a bride 18 or older, the ages to marry in India. We don’t show anyone younger as a rishta.
  • You must be free to marry, and say your marital status truthfully. If you are awaiting a divorce, say so: you can marry again only after the court’s decree.
  • Married people, and anyone who doesn’t want a rishta profile, can use the kundli, panchang and family features with a kundli-only account. It has no profile card, so nobody finds it.
  • You may use the App only for personal, lawful purposes. Give true information and keep your account secure.

2) One account, one rishta profile

  • An account manages one rishta profile: your own, or one you make for a family member or friend.
  • Keep it true and up to date. When the person is no longer looking, for example after finding a match, hide or delete the profile.

3) Making a profile for someone else

  • You may make a profile for your son, daughter, brother, sister, relative or friend only if they know about it and agree to it, and only to find them a match for marriage. You confirm this when you make it, and we record your declaration with the date.
  • Use only true details, and photos of that person that they are happy to share. Add caste, Manglik, income or other personal details only if they are happy for you to share them.
  • The profile shows who manages it (for example “Managed by a parent”).
  • We may ask the person in the profile to do the face check, and we remove profiles made without their agreement.
  • The person can ask us to remove the profile at any time, even without an account. Is there a profile of you that you didn’t agree to? Report it with “Made without this person’s OK” or write to support@shaadiparrot.com (no account needed), or use shaadiparrot.com/help.html#remove-profile.

4) What we check, and what we don’t

  • Every photo is checked automatically for safety before it appears on a profile.
  • A “Verified” badge means the person passed a live face check that matched their profile photos. Profiles made by family members may not have one.
  • We do not check documents, marital status, education, work, income, family details, caste or anything else people write about themselves.
  • Before any step towards marriage, check the person and their family yourself. See Safety tips.

5) What’s not allowed

You must not post, send or share:

  • nudity or sexual content;
  • anything involving minors, or a profile for anyone under 18 (or a groom under 21);
  • impersonation, fake profiles, or profiles made without the person’s agreement;
  • other people’s photos, or photos you have no right to use;
  • hate, harassment or threats, including caste, religious and community slurs;
  • dowry demands (dowry is illegal in India);
  • requests for money, gifts, OTPs or bank details, and any scam;
  • phone numbers, e-mails, social media handles or other contact details in your profile text;
  • spam, ads or promotion;
  • anything else that is illegal.

You are responsible for what you post. Face verification must be done by the person in the profile, with their own face and photos; we may refuse or remove a Verified badge if the photos aren’t theirs, or if one person verifies more than one account.

6) Kundli, horoscopes and Mithu

  • Kundli matching, horoscopes, the panchang, good dates and Mithu’s readings follow Vedic astrology. Astrology is a tradition, not a prediction or a guarantee. Don’t base health, legal or money decisions on it.
  • Mithu’s chat answers, daily horoscopes, forecasts and kundli match readings are written with the help of AI and can be wrong.
  • We don’t sell remedies, pujas, gemstones or amulets.

7) Safety

We can’t guarantee how other members behave. Follow the Safety tips in the App, meet with family, and never send money. If something feels wrong, block and report.

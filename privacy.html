Privacy policy

What Shaadi Parrot collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information.

Last updated: 2026-09-29

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what data Shaadi Parrot (the “App”) collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: your phone number, used for sign-in and safety. It is never shown to other users.
  • Profile: name, date of birth and age, gender, who you’re looking for, photos, bio, interests, languages, lifestyle, relationship goals and, only if you choose, religion and community.
  • Astrology: birth date, and optionally birth time and birth place, used for compatibility, Daily Fates and horoscopes.
  • Location: your device location is used to show distance. Other users only see an approximate distance and your city, never your exact position.
  • Photos and safety checks: profile photos are checked automatically for safety using image analysis (Google Cloud Vision). If you use selfie verification once it is available, those selfies are kept private and used only to confirm it’s you.
  • Messages and safety: chats and reports may be processed to detect abuse, spam, scams and policy violations.
  • Parrot AI chat: messages you send to Parrot, together with the relevant parts of your profile, are sent to our AI provider, DeepSeek, to generate replies. Please don’t share passwords, bank details or other sensitive information with Parrot.
  • Usage, device and diagnostics: which screens you open and key actions (for example swipes, matches and messages sent, but never message text), purchase outcomes, crash reports, device identifiers and notification tokens. These are collected with Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics and linked to your account ID so we can fix problems and improve the App.
  • Advertising: we show rewarded ads (which you choose to watch, for example to get a reward) and full-screen interstitial ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device’s advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in Android settings. Parrot+ removes full-screen ads.

2. How we use data

We use data to provide matching, personalize your experience, enable messaging, prevent fraud and abuse, improve performance, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing

We share limited data only with service providers that help operate the App:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: hosting, sign-in, database, storage, notifications, analytics, crash reports and image safety checks.
  • Google AdMob: ads.
  • Google Play: payments.
  • DeepSeek: generating Parrot AI replies.

Other users see your public profile card: name, age, photos, bio, city, approximate distance and the details you choose to show.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users’ safety and rights.

When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photos, likes, matches, chats and settings. Limited records (for example, a one-way hash of your phone number to prevent ban evasion) may be kept for safety.

4. Where your data is processed

Shaadi Parrot is operated by DeLuca Family Universe LLC, a company in the United States. Your data is stored and processed on Google Cloud and Firebase servers in the United States and the European Union. Parrot AI messages are processed by DeepSeek, whose servers may be located outside India, including in China.

We transfer data outside India only as the law allows, share with each provider only what it needs for its task, and protect your data as described in this policy wherever it is processed.

5. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • Edit profile information in the App.
  • Adjust permissions (for example, location) in your device settings.
  • Request account deletion in Settings. See how to delete your account.

Depending on your region, you may have additional rights such as access, correction and deletion.

6. Retention

We keep data only as long as necessary for providing the service, safety, and legal compliance. Some records may be retained for fraud prevention and dispute resolution where permitted by law.

7. Children

The App is not for anyone under 18. If you believe a minor is using the App, report their profile in the App or write to support@shaadiparrot.com right away.

8. Grievances and your rights in India

If you are in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you the right to access a summary of your data, correct or update it, have it erased, withdraw consent and nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. You can edit your profile in the App and delete your account in Settings › Delete my account.

For any other request or complaint about your data, write to our Grievance Officer, Arsen Khasiev, at support@shaadiparrot.com with the subject “Privacy grievance”. We acknowledge requests within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Contact

Questions? Contact us at support@shaadiparrot.com.

Company: DeLuca Family Universe LLC (operator of Shaadi Parrot)
Governing law: the laws of India for users in India; the laws of the United States for everyone else. Nothing in this policy limits the rights you have under the law where you live.

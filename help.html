Getting started

What is Shaadi Parrot?

A matrimony app for people looking to marry and their families, with kundli matching, the panchang and Mithu, our astrology parrot. Every rishta profile shows its kundli match with yours, and every morning Mithu picks up to three verified rishtas for you.

Is Shaadi Parrot only for marriage?

Yes, it’s a matrimony app. Rishtas shows only people looking to marry: grooms 21 or older and brides 18 or older. You can make the profile for yourself, or for your son, daughter, brother, sister, relative or friend with their agreement, and it reads like a biodata: religion, mother tongue, education, work and family details. Profiles made by family say who manages them.

Who can join?

Anyone 18 or older. A groom needs to be 21 or older for a rishta profile. You sign in with your phone number or your Google account; neither is ever shown to other people.

We’re already married. Is it for us?

Yes. Choose “Already married?” when you sign up, and Shaadi Parrot becomes your kundli and family app: the panchang, good dates, kundlis and a daily line for everyone at home. A kundli-only account has no profile card, so nobody finds it.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Shaadi Parrot is on Android, on Google Play.

What language is the app in?

English, for now.

Rishtas and matching

What are Today’s rishtas?

Every morning Mithu picks up to three verified people for you, each with 18 or more gunas with you. You open one a day and read it: your kundli match, what you share and the parrot’s verdict. Then you send an interest or skip it for now.

How are rishtas ranked?

By your partner preferences: the best fits come first. A preference you mark “Must have” hides people outside it both ways. Other members never see your preferences. More in the privacy policy.

What does “Why you match” mean?

Profiles come with a short note on what you have in common, so you know why someone was shown to you before you send an interest.

How do interests work?

An interest tells a rishta you’d like to talk. If they accept, it’s a match and you can chat. Seeing and accepting the interests you receive is free. Free members can send a number of interests a day; Parrot+ removes the limit.

What are crowns and boosts?

A crown tells someone you’re really interested and helps you stand out. A boost puts your profile in front of more people for a while. Both come in packs through Google Play.

Mithu and astrology

What can Mithu do?

Answer your questions about a kundli, a rishta, good dates and family; he reads the kundli before he answers. His full reading of a kundli match comes with Parrot+, or as a single reading. Messages you send to Mithu are processed by our AI provider to write his answers.

Do you do kundli matching?

Yes. Every rishta profile shows your Guna Milan out of 36 with them, and in the Kundli tab you can match any two people: all 8 kootas, and Nadi, Bhakoot and Manglik dosha with their cancellations. On this site, Kundli match is free too.

How does the horoscope work?

We use the Vedic (sidereal) zodiac with the Lahiri ayanamsa and put the Moon and your nakshatra first. Your reading arrives every morning at 7:30 AM IST. Your birth date is enough to start; birth time and place make it more precise.

Do I have to believe in astrology?

No. Kundli and horoscopes are a tradition, not a verdict on people. Who you talk to is always your call.

Safety and privacy

Is there a profile of you that you didn’t agree to?

You don’t need an account. Write to support@shaadiparrot.com with the subject “Remove a profile”, the name on the profile and its city, and a screenshot if you have one.

We acknowledge within 24 hours and decide within 7 days, and photos used without your OK are removed within 2 hours of a valid complaint, as our Terms say. Members can also report it in the app with “Made without this person’s OK”.

What do other people see about me?

Signed-in members see your rishta profile: name, age, photos (blurred if you keep them private), city, a few lines about you and the biodata details you added. Never your phone number, e-mail, birth date, time or place, or exact location.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile or your chat, tap Report or Block. Our team reviews every report, and a report of nudity, of someone who looks under 18 or of photos used without their OK hides the profile at once. See the safety centre for more.

How does face verification work?

The front camera opens in the app. You keep your face in the oval and follow Mithu: look straight, then two quick moves like a turn or a smile. The app takes the photos by itself, and our server checks that it’s you in your profile photos. It takes about 20 seconds. Verified people get a badge and Today’s rishtas.

Who sees my face check photos?

Other users never do. Usually no one does: the check is automatic. If it isn’t sure, a person on our team takes one more look. Two of the three photos are deleted right away, and you can remove the rest in Me: tap your Verified badge, then Manage › Remove my verification. Details are in the privacy policy.

Do you sell my data?

No. Read the privacy policy for what we collect and why.

Account and billing

Is Shaadi Parrot free?

Yes. Making a profile, seeing and accepting interests, kundli matching, the panchang and the daily horoscope are free. Parrot+ is an optional plan with unlimited interests, as many questions to Mithu as you like, his full reading for every kundli match and every good date with its time window. Compare free and Parrot+.

How do I cancel Parrot+?

In Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Parrot+ stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel it.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Me › Settings › Delete my account. Without the app: request deletion here.

Safety centre

What we do to protect you and your family, what you can do, and the rules everyone on Shaadi Parrot agrees to.

How to reportGuidelines

What we do

Safety is built into the app, not bolted on.

  • Your phone number is never shown. It is only used for signing in and safety.
  • Your location is rounded to about 2 km. People see your city and a rough distance, never where you are.
  • Every photo gets an automatic check before it appears on a profile.
  • Verified means a live face check. The badge shows the person passed a live camera check that matches their own photos.
  • Profiles made by family say so, for example “Managed by a parent”, and need the person’s agreement.
  • Chats and reports are checked for abuse, spam, scams and rule-breaking.
  • Report or block anyone from their profile or your chat. Our team reviews every report, and a serious one hides the profile at once.
  • Adults only. Everyone is 18 or older, and a groom on Rishtas is 21 or older.

Stay safe

Scammers target matrimony apps too. If something feels off, trust your instincts and talk it over with your family before you do anything.

Money and scams

  • Never send money, gift cards or OTPs to anyone you met here, whatever the story: a hospital bill, a customs fee for a gift, a visa, a ticket or a business problem.
  • Be careful with “NRI” or “settled abroad” profiles that talk of marriage quickly and then need money for customs, a visa or travel. A real rishta never needs your money.
  • Never share OTP codes, passwords, UPI PINs or bank details. Shaadi Parrot will never ask for them.
  • Be careful with links, and with people who want to move to another app quickly.

Dowry

Dowry is illegal in India, and so is asking for it. If anyone asks for dowry, report them in the App.

Check the family

  • We check photos and faces, not documents, marital status, education, work or income.
  • Before any commitment, check the person and the family yourself: talk to the parents, confirm the job and the city, and ask people you trust.

Meeting

  • Meet first in a public place, with family or someone you trust, and tell others where you’re going.
  • Arrange your own transport and keep your phone charged.
  • Leave at once if anything feels wrong.

Online, and if something goes wrong

Online

  • Never share intimate photos.
  • If someone threatens to share your photos or messages, don’t pay. Keep screenshots and report it.

If you are cheated or threatened

  • Call the national cybercrime helpline 1930, or report it at cybercrime.gov.in. In an emergency, call 112.
  • Keep screenshots of the chat and the profile.

Adults only, and our child safety standards

Shaadi Parrot is only for adults. If you think someone is under 18, report the profile right away: a report that someone looks under 18 hides it at once while our team checks.

What we never allow

  • Anyone under 18 using the App, and any rishta profile for a bride under 18 or a groom under 21. Child marriage is illegal in India.
  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation in any form: images, videos, text, grooming, sextortion or trafficking. Zero tolerance.
  • Children never have profiles. Parents can add a child to My family for their kundli, baby names and good dates; it stays private in the parent’s account.

What we do

  • Everyone confirms they are 18 or older when they sign in, and grooms must be 21 or older to have a rishta profile.
  • Every photo is checked automatically before it appears on a profile.
  • A report of someone who looks under 18, or of nudity, hides the profile at once and alerts our team.
  • We remove the content, ban the accounts involved and report child sexual abuse material and other illegal content to the authorities as the law requires.
  • To reach us about child safety, write to support@shaadiparrot.com with the subject “Child safety”. You don’t need an account. If a child is in danger, call 112 or Childline 1098.

Community guidelines

Shaadi Parrot is a respectful place for people looking to marry and their families. These rules apply to profiles, photos, chats and everything you send.

We expect you to

  • Be honest: true details, your own recent photos, and say who manages the profile.
  • Make a profile for someone else only if they know about it and agree.
  • Treat every family with respect, including when you say no.
  • Respect every religion, caste and community.
  • Keep chats polite, and share contact details only when you choose to.

We do not allow

  • Nudity or sexual content.
  • Anything involving minors (zero tolerance).
  • Fake profiles, impersonation, or someone else’s photos.
  • Profiles made without the person’s agreement.
  • Harassment, bullying, threats or hate speech.
  • Caste, religious or community slurs or insults, anywhere on your profile or in chats.
  • Dowry demands. Dowry is illegal in India.
  • Asking for money, gifts, OTPs or bank details, and scams.
  • Phone numbers, e-mails or social media handles in your profile text.
  • Spam, ads, and anything illegal.

Reporting and enforcement. If someone breaks these rules, report and block them. A report of nudity, of someone who looks under 18, or of someone’s photos used without their OK hides the profile at once until our team decides. We may remove content, restrict features or ban accounts to protect members. The full rules are in our Terms of Service.

How to report and block

  • Open the person’s profile or your chat with them, tap Report, choose what happened and send.
  • Block them if you don’t want to hear from them again. They won’t be told.
  • A report of nudity, of someone who looks under 18, or of someone’s photos used without their OK hides the profile at once until our team decides.
  • Is there a profile of you that you didn’t agree to? Report it with “Made without this person’s OK” or write to support@shaadiparrot.com (no account needed), or use shaadiparrot.com/help.html#remove-profile.
  • You can also write to support@shaadiparrot.com.

Reaching us

Write to support@shaadiparrot.com. For complaints under Indian law, use the subject “Grievance” and include your registered phone number or e-mail, the profile or content concerned and a short description.

We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and decide them within 7 days. Unlawful content is removed within 36 hours of a valid complaint, and content showing nudity, a sexual act or impersonation within 2 hours. Same timelines as our Terms, section 12.
